# 14 · Driver list

For a match day the team plans who drives and who rides along. The distance from the meeting point to the venue is calculated automatically and credited to the drivers (F-20, issue #59, decision E-82). Out of scope: reimbursement in francs, routes with pick-up stops, parents as drivers (needs the parent functions).

Status legend: see [README](../../README.md#requirement-status).

## List

| ID | Requirement | Status |
|---|---|---|
| DRV-01 | Every match day has the section «Fahrerliste» in `/termine/:id`. It can be used as soon as meeting point and venue are set; until then it says what is missing («Es fehlt: Treffpunkt und Spielort.»). Other events have no driver list (API 409). | Decided |
| DRV-02 | Every member of a team of the match day (any team role) enters herself as driver with the number of free seats (1 to 8) and removes herself again. A person drives at most one car per match day. Since E-98 every member of the team also enters, changes and removes drivers on behalf of others («Plätze zuweisen», DRV-32); coaches (of any team, E-69) and admins too. A driver whose car has passengers is taken off only after a question («<Name> austragen?»); the passengers lose their seats and are notified (DRV-11). After the end of the match day only coaches and admins still change the list. | Decided (criteria, E-98), Assumption (1 to 8 seats; every member may drive, also people who are not called up; after the end only coaches and admins) |
| DRV-03 | Passengers: members add themselves to a car with a free seat and leave it again. Coaches and admins assign and move passengers. A person sits in at most one car (whoever joins another car moves; whoever becomes driver leaves her seat). A car never has more passengers than seats; seats cannot be reduced below the passengers. Concurrent requests for the last seat: one gets it, the other 409. | Decided |
| DRV-04 | The list shows who has no seat yet: with a published call-up the called-up players, otherwise the players who responded «dabei»; without those who drive or ride along. | Decided |

## Route and credits

| ID | Requirement | Status |
|---|---|---|
| DRV-05 | The route from the meeting point to the venue is calculated once per match day: distance in kilometres (one decimal) and driving time in minutes. Each driver is credited with the return trip (twice the distance). | Decided (criteria), Assumption (calculated on the first view of the list) |
| DRV-06 | The calculation sits behind an interface (`Routing`). Profiles `dev` and `test` use a simulator with fixed values per pair of places, controllable through `PUT /api/test/routing`. Production uses OpenRouteService (directions `driving-car`, geocoding in Switzerland), tested with recorded answers; its key comes from the environment variable `STV_ORS_API_KEY`. | Decided (criteria), Assumption (OpenRouteService, see O-63) |
| DRV-07 | Routing unavailable or no result: the list works without distance and shows a hint. Coaches and admins enter the kilometres by hand («Kilometer erfassen»); a manual value is marked «von Hand erfasst». «Strecke neu berechnen» asks the service again and replaces a manual value. An unavailable service is asked again on the next view after 10 minutes; «no result» stays until the places change or someone recalculates. | Decided (criteria), Assumption (retry after 10 minutes, manual value replaced by recalculating) |
| DRV-08 | Changing meeting point or venue recalculates the route and therefore the credits (on the next view of the list or the summary). A manual value applies only to the places it was entered for. | Decided |
| DRV-09 | The page «Fahrten» (`/fahrten`) shows per season the number of trips and the credited kilometres per person. Coaches and admins see everyone who drove, everyone else only themselves. Upcoming match days count as soon as a car is entered; trips without known distance count as trips without kilometres. | Decided (criteria), Assumption (upcoming match days count; only drivers, not passengers) |
| DRV-10 | A cancelled match day keeps its list, but credits no kilometres and counts no trips. Its list can no longer be changed. | Decided |

## Season plan

| ID | Requirement | Status |
|---|---|---|
| DRV-14 | The page «Fahrerliste» (`/teams/:id/fahrerliste`) shows the season as a grid: the people of the team down, the match days across (E-98). A tap on a cell enters the person as driver of that match day or takes her off; members change their own row, coaches and admins every row. Per match day the rules of DRV-01 and DRV-02 apply. The form «Fahrten einplanen» of E-92 is gone; the API to enter one driver for several match days at once stays. | Decided |
| DRV-15 | The real routing service is the first one that is configured: Google Maps (Routes API) with `STV_GOOGLE_MAPS_API_KEY`, OpenRouteService with `STV_ORS_API_KEY`, otherwise OpenStreetMap without a key (Nominatim finds places, OSRM calculates the route). So a route is calculated without any configuration. (Replaces the second half of DRV-06: production no longer depends on an OpenRouteService key.) | Decided |
| DRV-16 | If the meeting point names a venue the app already knows with coordinates (e.g. the home hall delivered by swiss unihockey), those coordinates are used. Otherwise the keyless geocoder searches the text and, if it finds nothing, the text without its first words (down to the town). | Assumption |
| DRV-17 | The season plan shows how the trips are shared: per member of the team the trips (done and planned) and the credited kilometres, in the column on the right of the grid («Fahrten», «km») with a total in the last row; the list «Wer kann fahren» shows done and planned trips (DRV-34). | Decided |

## Proposal and travelling on one's own

| ID | Requirement | Status |
|---|---|---|
| DRV-18 | Every person says whether she can drive and how many places her car usually has: «Kann fahren» (default **yes**) and «Plätze im Auto» including herself, 2 to 9, default **5** (herself and four others). The person changes it in her profile; coaches (of any team) and admins change it for everyone on the page «Fahrerliste der Saison» («Wer kann fahren»). The values are defaults for the proposal and for entering a car; the number of seats of a car on a match day can still be changed (DRV-02). | Decided (per player, default 5), Assumption (default «yes», per person and not per team) |
| DRV-19 | «Vorschlag erarbeiten»: coaches and admins let the app propose a driver list for a match day. Everyone who needs a seat is seated: the players without a seat (DRV-04) and the coaches who responded «dabei», without those who travel on their own (DRV-20). Cars that are already entered stay and are filled first. Further drivers are chosen among the people who travel anyway and can drive (DRV-18), the one with the fewest trips in the season first (trips that are over plus planned ones, DRV-17), then by name; each brings her usual number of places. As few cars as needed are proposed. The proposal is shown first and changes nothing; «Vorschlag übernehmen» enters it, with the usual notifications (DRV-11). If there are not enough drivers, the proposal says who is left without a seat. | Decided, changed by E-97 (on the page «Fahrerliste» «Alle verteilen» seats everyone; the proposal dialog is not shown) |
| DRV-20 | «Reist individuell an»: a member marks herself as travelling on her own, in the driver list or with her response ([ANM-13](04-responses.md)); coaches and admins mark others. Such a person is listed under «Reist individuell an», needs no seat (not in «Noch ohne Platz», not in the proposal) and leaves a seat she had. A driver cannot be marked until her car is removed. Whoever enters a car or takes a seat is no longer marked. | Decided |

## Driving time, season page, swaps and other trips (E-92)

| ID | Requirement | Status |
|---|---|---|
| DRV-21 | The driving time of a match day is calculated automatically (DRV-05), but coaches (of any team) and admins can overwrite it in minutes («Fahrzeit anpassen», 1 to 600). An overwritten time is marked «angepasst» and is used everywhere instead of the calculated one: driver list, season page and timeline (TML-04, TML-06, TML-07). «Automatisch berechnen» removes it. It also stays when the route is recalculated, and it is dropped when meeting point or venue change (like the kilometres entered by hand, DRV-08). With kilometres entered by hand a time can be entered too. | Decided (overwritable), Assumption (range, kept on recalculation) |
| DRV-22 | An info button next to the driving time («So wird die Fahrzeit berechnet») explains it: which service calculated it (Google Maps, OpenRouteService, OpenStreetMap, or the simulator in test mode), whether day and time of departure were taken into account (the services the app uses calculate without traffic, so: «nein, ohne Verkehr»), when it was calculated (date and time), and whether the time was overwritten by hand and by whom. | Decided (content), Assumption (no traffic) |
| DRV-23 | On the match day (`/termine/:id`) the section «Fahrerliste» shows by default only how many places the cars have for how many people travel together: «<n> Plätze für <m> Personen», with the number of cars and whether places are missing. People who travel on their own do not count (DRV-20). The button «Plätze zuweisen», which everyone of the team can press, opens the cars as bubbles (DRV-32). Travelling on one's own stays: for oneself, and for coaches and admins also for others. A link «Zur Fahrerliste» leads to the season page of the team. Route and driving time (DRV-07, DRV-21) are still set there by coaches and admins. | Decided (E-98) |
| DRV-24 | The page «Fahrerliste» (`/fahrerliste`, entry from the start page, from the team and from every match day) is where the season is planned (DRV-14). With several own teams a choice «Team» switches between them. A tap on a match day in the grid opens its cars below, as on the match day behind «Plätze zuweisen» (DRV-32). `/teams/:id/fahrerliste` stays as the address of a team. | Decided (E-98) |
| DRV-25 | **Swap («Tauschen»).** A driver offers her trip of a match day to another member of the team, either as a swap against a trip of the other person on another match day, or as a takeover without a trip in return. She either asks («Tausch anfragen»): the other person gets a notification (`DRIVER_LIST_CHANGED`) and accepts or declines on the page, the asking person can withdraw; or she enters it directly («Direkt tauschen»), for example when they agreed in the dressing room: the other person is notified. Coaches and admins can do both for any driver. A swap moves only the driver: seats and passengers of the cars stay. Whoever becomes the driver loses a seat as passenger and her mark «Reist individuell an» on that match day. Not possible: someone who already drives on that match day, a match day that is over (except for coaches and admins) or cancelled. If the cars changed since the request, accepting fails with a message. | Later (E-98: no page; the API and the data stay) |
| DRV-26 | **History («Verlauf»).** The season page lists the changes of the driver plan, newest first: a trip entered («Eingetragen»), taken out, swapped, taken over, a swap asked for, declined or withdrawn, another trip added or removed (DRV-28). Each entry says who did it and when, e.g. «Petra Meier hat mit Sara Brun getauscht: Petra fährt am Sa 05.12., Sara am So 22.11.» · «21.11.2026, 14:03». Changes from before the history existed are not listed. | Later (E-98: no page; the API and the data stay) |
| DRV-27 | The table «Verteilung der Fahrten» (DRV-17) also counts the other trips (DRV-28) and ends with a total row («Total»): trips that are over, planned trips, all trips and kilometres. | Later (E-98: no page; the API and the data stay) |
| DRV-28 | **Other trips («Weitere Fahrten»).** Trips for something that is not an event in the app (a tournament, a club outing) can be added on the season page so that they count: date, what it was («Wofür», 1 to 80 characters), driver and kilometres one way (0.1 to 2000). The driver is credited with the return trip, like on a match day (DRV-05). Members add their own trips, coaches and admins also for others; the driver, whoever added it, coaches and admins remove it. They count in the distribution (DRV-27) and on «Fahrten» (DRV-09): over once the date has passed, planned before. | Later (E-98: no page; the API and the data stay) |

## Simple planning (E-98)

The driver list is simple and serves two cases: planning the season, and the last changes right before a match day. Of the design proposals in [docs/proposals/driver-list.md](../proposals/driver-list.md) proposal B «Raster» was chosen (E-98).

| ID | Requirement | Status |
|---|---|---|
| DRV-29 | **Expected people.** The plan assumes how many people travel to a match day: by default all players and coaches of the team. Coaches (of any team) and admins set another number for the team in the season (1 to 60) and, if needed, for a single match day. From this number and the places of the cars the plan knows whether a match day has enough cars. | Decided (E-98), Assumption (the number includes the drivers) |
| DRV-30 | **Share fairly («Fair verteilen»).** Coaches and admins let the app propose the drivers that are still missing on the upcoming match days: each match day gets drivers until there is a place for everyone who is expected. Drivers who are entered stay and count; the proposal never changes them. The next driver is the member who can drive (DRV-18) with the fewest trips in the season (done and planned), then with fewer kilometres, then the one who has not driven for longer, then by name; nobody who responded «nicht dabei». Home match days (the meeting point is the venue) need no drivers. The proposal is shown first, as dashed cars in the grid, with the places it would give and, in the column on the right, the trips and kilometres of every person and the total as they would be once it is accepted («Mit Vorschlag», the values so far below as «bisher»); nothing is entered. «Vorschlag übernehmen» enters it, and from then on its trips stay like any other: a later «Fair verteilen» only adds. «Verwerfen» drops it. Every new driver gets one notification with her match days, instead of one per match day. | Decided (E-98), Assumption (order of the drivers) |
| DRV-31 | **Next in line («Wer ist dran»).** For one match day the app names the next driver by the same order as DRV-30. Coaches and admins add one more car this way. A driver who cannot drive hands her trip to the next in line with one tap («Ich kann nicht fahren»); coaches and admins do it for any driver. Seats and passengers of the car stay, the new driver is notified (like a direct takeover, DRV-25). If nobody is left, the app says so. | Assumption |
| DRV-32 | **Assign places («Plätze zuweisen»).** The cars of a match day show as bubbles: per car the driver, the people in it and the free seats; below, everyone who still needs a seat. Every member of the team arranges them for everyone of the team, coaches and admins too: tap a person, then the car she rides in; take her out; change the seats of a car (never fewer than the people in it); add a driver («Fahrerin oder Fahrer hinzufügen», with her usual free seats); take a driver off (with passengers only after a question, DRV-02); «Alle verteilen» seats everyone at once (the proposal of DRV-19, entered right away). Coaches and admins also mark someone who waits for a seat as travelling on her own (DRV-20). The same bubbles show on the match day behind «Plätze zuweisen» and on the page «Fahrerliste» below the grid. A cancelled match day, or one that is over for members, shows the cars without changes. | Decided (E-98), Assumption (every member arranges for everyone) |
| DRV-33 | **Remove all («Alle entfernen»).** Coaches and admins take every driver off the upcoming match days that are not cancelled, after a question that says how many trips on how many match days go. Passengers lose their seats. Every driver and passenger affected gets one notification. Match days that are over keep their cars. | Decided (E-98) |
| DRV-34 | **The grid.** Per person the places of her car (driver included, DRV-18) or «fährt nicht»; per match day the date, the town and the one-way distance in kilometres; on the right per person the trips (done and planned) and the credited kilometres, with a total; at the foot per match day the places of all cars against the people expected (green: enough, red: too few). | Decided (E-98) |

## Notes

- Only kilometres are counted, no money (assumption to confirm from the issue).
- Configuration: none is required. `STV_GOOGLE_MAPS_API_KEY` switches to Google Maps (needs a Google Cloud project with a billing account; the Routes API has a free monthly quota), `STV_ORS_API_KEY` to OpenRouteService. The OpenStreetMap services (`STV_NOMINATIM_URL`, `STV_OSRM_URL`) are free and ask for moderate use and an identifying user agent (`STV_ROUTING_USER_AGENT`); the app asks once per match day (O-64).
- The routing service is called outside of the database transaction; the result is stored per match day (table `route`).
